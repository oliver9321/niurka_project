# Product: Greentech SRL Corporate Website

Greentech SRL is a building technology and systems integration company based in the Dominican Republic. This is their corporate marketing website — a single-page application showcasing their services, partners, and contact information.

## Core Offerings
- Building Management Systems (BMS)
- Fire Protection (PCI / Protección Contra Incendios)
- Security (CCTV, Access Control)
- Guest Room Management (GRMS)
- Infrastructure (Networking, Fiber Optic)
- Audio & Video systems
- Maintenance & Post-sale support

## Site Sections
Hero → Stats → About (Misión & Visión) → Especialidades → Solutions (Bento Grid) → BMS Dashboard Showcase → Partners Marquee → Testimonials → Contact Form → Footer + WhatsApp floating button

## Language
All user-facing copy is in **Spanish**. Code identifiers mix English and Spanish.

## Design Archetype
Swiss / High-Contrast, adapted for engineering/tech. Clean, generous whitespace, no visual overload. The full design system is defined in `design_guidelines.json` at the project root.
